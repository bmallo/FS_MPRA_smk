One spot: M molecules are often unmarked across oligo 60–86

At oligo 60–86, 10.9% (run 3), 5.6% (run 1) and 7.1% (HEK293T) of M molecules have no m6A across the stretch. The shuffle predicts 0.1%.

It spans the 60–64 A/T run, which Hia5 cannot mark. The stretch is set by the flanking adenines (65, 70, 71) going unmarked together.

Cost to the caller: if a fraction f of M is bound at an adenine, an unmarked call there counts less. The median weight is 1.87; it falls to 1.01 at f = 25% and 0.55 at f = 50%.

Source: a6_proxies/summary/*/clustering.tsv.gz (M, spans ≥ 20 bp), llr_weight.tsv (HepG2 run-3 emission table) · weight = ln((1 − p_p) / (1 − p_a))

Post hoc check (not in the fixed settings): near A/T runs of ≥ 4, M shows more clustering than elsewhere (median excess 5.5% vs 1.7% in HepG2 run 3, 6.6% vs 1.8% in HEK293T); naked DNA shows a small rise (1.6% vs 0.7%). So part of this spot may be how the caller handles A-runs, and part protein; the caller differs between the two, so B1 must settle it. Run 3 (5 min) shows twice the excess of run 1 (10 min).
