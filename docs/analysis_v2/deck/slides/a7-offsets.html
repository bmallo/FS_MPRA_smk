Step A7 · One dropped A/T

Losing one adenine reshapes calls nearby more than it adds or removes them

Each of the 101 element A/T positions dropped in turn (what any SNV or deletion there does for the caller).

Per edit, largest change (C): median 0.9% (HepG2) and 1.0% (HEK293T) of molecules; 90th percentile 2.2–2.4%; maximum 4.2–5.0%. In M: median 0.2–0.3%.

Reach: beyond ±5 bp the median largest change is 0.4%, maximum 2.5–3.1%.

Net called bp per molecule hardly moves (median −0.006 to −0.017 bp): boundaries shift and calls move between size classes.

Source: a7_snv_edits/*/artifacts.npz, edits.tsv (kind = drop) · curves are means over the 101 drops; the text gives per-edit ranges

Why the dip at 0: calls start and end on observed A/T, so removing one moves a boundary off it. Small 10–19 bp calls fall a few bp away while 20–40 bp and nucleosome-sized calls rise: losing a marked adenine lets neighbouring protected stretches merge into a longer call.
