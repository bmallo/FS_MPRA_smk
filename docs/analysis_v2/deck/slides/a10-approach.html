Step A10 · Variant effects, per cell

Which SNVs change protection, where, and by how much?

Why

This is the step every earlier one prepared: compare each variant with WT using the calibrated test (A9), the caller-artifact masks (A7) and the detection limits (A8), one cell at a time.

What we did

HepG2: every SNV vs WT at each adenine (primary) and each position × footprint size (secondary); one global test per SNV, then where. SNVs that drop their own A/T are tested apart. Checked with a ±5 bp mask and an empirical null. HEK293T: SNVs pooled per motif, clone vs clone.

What we found

  • HepG2: most SNVs change something (602 of 633; 467 with a ±5 bp mask), mostly within 20 bp.
  • Every change above 15 points is within 10 bp of its SNV: possibly Hia5 sequence preference.
  • 17 SNVs at oligo 143–151 raise protection across the whole element.

Source: results/analysis_v2_20261028/a10_effects (tools/analysis_v2/a10_effects.py, a10_sensitivity.py) · settings in rules.json (variant_effects) before results; post hoc items flagged

Effects are relative to in-cell WT molecules (A6), never absolute occupancy. HepG2 run 3 only (A8: runs differ). 633 SNVs, 1.35M molecules, median ~800 clones per SNV. HEK293T: 197 SNVs, mostly one clone each.
