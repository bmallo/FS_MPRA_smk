Step A8 · Spike-ins by labeling

Sensitivity is high where a molecule is labeled; low-labeled molecules are already "called"

Detected = a call covers the spike centre and at least half the spike. Net = detected after spiking − before, same molecules.

M: net 95–98% at every size, 10–147 bp.

C: 82–86% of low-labeled (0.01–0.2) molecules already carry a call over any given spot, so net there is only 11–19%. Where nothing was called before, detection is 88–100% in every group (post hoc).

75% protection (leaky spike): M 54–62% (HepG2), 53–59% (HEK293T).

Source: a8_confidence/spikes/*/spikes.tsv · position-specific emissions, perfect spikes, medians over positions · "nothing called before" = net / (1 − before)

FiberHMM's hexamer emissions in the same scan: M 95–98% (same), C net 2–4 points lower, and slightly more molecules already called (60% vs 57% in HepG2 C). Collateral (calls changing more than 10 bp outside the spike): median 2–7% of C molecules, up to 12–15%, from neighbouring calls merging with the spike.
