Step A11 · Putting Repeat 3 together

One site, three readouts that line up

At the site

SNVs that break the Sp1 match at 143–151 lower TF-size (10–40 bp) footprint coverage there by 0.2–2.3 points (A11 dose-response).

Across the element

17 SNVs inside the site raise the unmarked fraction element-wide, every allele at 146, 148 and 149 (A10); the shift tracks nucleosome-size calls.

Expression

The same 17 SNVs: median −2.59 log2 (LDLR) and −3.23 (LDLR.2), about 6–9-fold lower, against −0.10 for all other SNVs.

One reading that fits all three: Sp1 bound at Repeat 3 keeps the promoter open; without it, nucleosome-like protection spreads and expression drops. It is a hypothesis from correlated, separate experiments. Testable with B2 (time course) and naked controls (B1).

Source: a10_effects (hit map, element-wide SNVs), a11_motifs_function (dose.tsv, summary.json region)

Repeats 1 and 3 are described as Sp1 sites in the LDLR literature (Südhof et al. 1987; Dawson et al. 1988). These data add a molecule-level view across SNVs: footprint loss at the site together with a rise in element-wide protection, measured in different molecules of each variant.
