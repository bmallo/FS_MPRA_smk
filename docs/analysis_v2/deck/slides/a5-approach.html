Step A5 · Methylation call QC

Can we trust each m6A call, and what else rides along with it?

Why

A footprint is a stretch of missing m6A. False calls hide footprints; uneven calling can fake them. Bacterial dam methylation would add m6A that Hia5 never made.

What we did

Read about 1 M plasmid reads per run. Measured calls in unlabeled (U) molecules as the false-positive rate, by element position and by pass count. Compared GATC adenines with matched controls, forward with reverse reads, and top with bottom strands.

What we found

  • About 0.3% false calls vs 67–69% in labeled molecules; up to 3% at a few positions.
  • No dam methylation at GATC.
  • Calls hold up at 10–14 passes, but unlabeled molecules get more passes.

Source: results/analysis_v2_20261028/a5_m6a_qc (tools/analysis_v2/a5_m6a_extract.py, a5_m6a_summary.py)

U and M use the rules.json classes: distal m6A ≤ 0.01 and ≥ 0.60, where distal is the backbone outside the element ±100 bp and the barcode ±25 bp. The element is counted from WT molecules only. U is not a true no-Hia5 control: its molecules may carry light labeling, so its call rate is an upper bound on the false-positive rate. B1 (no-Hia5 and PCR controls) will measure it directly. Filters: full length, MAPQ ≥ 20, rq ≥ 0.99; passes kept as a variable.
