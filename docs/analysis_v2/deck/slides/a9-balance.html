Step A9 · Covariate balance

SNV molecules look like WT molecules, as much as chance allows

Why it matters: if SNV molecules were, say, better labeled than WT, chromatin differences could come from the labeling, not the SNV.

HepG2: median |SMD| 0.015–0.018 for SNVs vs 0.015–0.016 for pseudo-variants. Beyond the chance 99th percentile: labeling 10, passes 14, strand 9, read quality 7 of 632 SNVs (~6 expected each).

HEK293T: 2, 3 and 1 of 152 SNVs beyond chance; wide spread because most SNVs have few clones.

Labeling is also removed by the standardisation; flagged SNVs are marked in A10.

Source: a9_statistics/*/balance.tsv · SMD = (SNV mean − WT mean) ÷ pooled SD · read quality from the HepG2 ledger only

No variant chromatin endpoint is involved here: these are read properties only. A run-level imbalance would show as a shift of the blue outline against the grey; none is visible.
