Step A10 · HepG2 · where the changes are

Changes cluster within 20 bp of the SNV; the biggest are within 10

Adenines (±5 bp mask): 32% of those 6–10 bp away are hits, 14% at 11–20, 7% at 21–50, 2.7% beyond 50.

Size: all 224 hits above 15 points (A7 mask) are within 10 bp, up to 53 points (25C>A at its neighbour). With the ±5 bp mask, all 45 such hits are 6–10 bp away; every allele at 73 raises oligo 65 by 35–37 points.

Hia5 marks an adenine differently depending on the bases around it, so large changes this close may be sequence, not protein. Only naked DNA of each variant (B1) can tell.
