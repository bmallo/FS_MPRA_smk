How to read it

Each view shows one cell line only; nothing from the other cell is drawn, overlaid or used. Coordinates are the 211-bp element in oligo order (21–231; genomic = 11,089,264 + oligo, GRCh38, chr19). The activity track is the published MPRA (LDLR and LDLR.2), the only functional readout, and is the same in both views.

HEK293T view: footprints K01–K23 are HEK293T's own map peaks (no relation to the HepG2 F numbers). Per-SNV values exist for 105 SNVs, each a single plasmid clone tested against the WT clone null; only endpoints that pass HEK293T's own calibration get significance marks. No per-SNV footprint tests or bias checks were run in HEK293T.

HepG2 view, per-SNV tracks put the three substitutions at each position in three stacked rows or as three dots. Effects are SNV minus WT, standardized over the 10 WT labeling strata (C_std). A filled or outlined mark is q < 0.05 in its own family.

Footprint rows: one per size-resolved footprint frozen from half of the WT barcodes. The pale bar is its span (centre ± length/2), shaded by WT occupancy. Cells are each SNV's occupancy change after the near-naked correction (ΔC_std − b·ΔM). Significance is post hoc on two families: A/T-retaining SNVs (plus A/T-changing SNVs too far away to touch a call) as the primary family, and A/T-changing SNVs within a footprint's call reach as a separate secondary family. Only the 12 footprints that pass the WT pseudo-variant calibration carry cells; the other six are drawn when "show uncalibrated" is on, without cells.

Bias check (table columns "obs / bias null"): for each footprint, molecules with no ≥ 60-bp call near it had the A/T bases within 20 bp of the span shuffled across molecules (same class and labeling stratum), then re-called. That keeps every per-base methylation rate, so enzyme sequence preference and labeling, and removes only same-molecule co-protection. A ratio near 1 means the calls are what per-base rates alone produce. Footprints ≥ 60 bp cannot be tested this way.

A substitution that removes an A/T inside a footprint also removes one methylatable base the caller reads, which by itself lowers short-call rates on near-naked molecules too. Rows tagged "A/T lost" should be read with that in mind.

Chromatin change location: for each SNV, the closest place any calibrated chromatin test changed at q < 0.05 (one BH family over 18,205 tests): at the SNV, within 30 bp, more than 30 bp away, or only in the element-wide accessibility/nucleosome scalars.
