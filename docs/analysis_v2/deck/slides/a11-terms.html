Step A11 · Terms used in this section

Motif scores, chromatin summaries and expression

TermMeaning
Motif score (log2-odds)How well a stretch of DNA matches a factor's preferred sequence (JASPAR matrix). Δ score = variant − WT at the WT best site; −8 to −10 ≈ site broken, 0 = untouched.
SiteThe WT best match of the matrix in or next to the element region (e.g. Sp1 at oligo 143–151).
Footprint change at a motifChange in how often 10–40 bp calls cover the site (points), SNV vs WT, ±5 bp mask.
Dose-response slopeFootprint change per unit of motif-score change, across the SNVs in that motif.
Element-wide shiftMean change in the unmarked fraction at adenines more than 50 bp from the SNV (points). It tracks nucleosome-size call coverage (r 0.87).
Local changeMean change in the unmarked fraction at adenines 11–50 bp from the SNV.
Expression effectPublished MPRA: log2 (variant / WT) RNA output in HepG2; LDLR and LDLR.2 are two measurements (r 0.97).
Within positionComparing only the alleles at the same position (3 per position), so motif location cannot create the association.
