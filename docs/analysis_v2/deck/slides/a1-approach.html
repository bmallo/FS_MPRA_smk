Step A1 · Data inventory

Before any analysis: what exactly did each run measure?

Why

Every comparison we make is only as clean as the runs behind it. If two runs differ in reaction, chemistry and m6A caller at once, a difference between them cannot be pinned on any one of these.

What we did

Read every BAM’s header (sequencing movie, chemistry, CCS, m6A caller, fibertools, aligner), counted reads and plasmid alignments, and listed the reaction conditions with their source. Then counted how many attributes differ for each pair of runs.

What we found

  • HepG2 runs 1 and 2 differ only in sequencing date: our one true replicate pair.
  • Run 3 differs from them in 7 ways, so 5 vs 10 min is confounded.
  • HEK293T differs from every HepG2 run in 8–9 ways.

Source: results/analysis_v2_20261028/a1_inventory (tools/analysis_v2/a1_inventory.py, config/analysis_v2/runs.json)

The inventory is a registry used by every later step. Reaction conditions for HepG2 come from the 14 Oct condition note (user-confirmed). For HEK293T, 10 min comes from the user; Hia5 volume and cells per reaction are not recorded anywhere we found.
