Follow-up · 60-86 · Labeling EXPLORATORY

Coverage rises with labeling, as a real footprint should; naked DNA falls to zero

Oligo 73: WT 2.3% → 6.0-6.3% as labeling rises; simulated naked 6.1% → 0% by labeling 0.5.

Why that matters: more labeling means more marks; false calls from too few marks vanish, while a real footprint is seen more often (A8 spikes).

Contrast, oligo 182 (most-covered 20-40 bp spot elsewhere, CREB1/CREM window): falls with labeling like the simulation.

Source: fp60_86/labeling.tsv · naked molecules: marking = q × naked relative rate by A-run class (A4 lookup), q fitted per labeling bin to WT marking elsewhere in the element

The simulation is coarse (A-run class only, all adenines read) and overshoots WT at low labeling, so read its shape, not its level. A6 found the same signal another way: 5.6-10.9% of M molecules have no m6A across 60-86, against 0.1% when marks are shuffled. The fall-off at 182 is worth its own look.
