Step A11 · Motif dose-response

Only the Repeat 3 Sp1 site shows a footprint that weakens as the motif breaks

Repeat 3 (Sp1, 143–151): +0.26 points per log2-odds unit (95% CI 0.08–0.44), q 0.017, 17 SNVs. Breaking the site lowers 10–40 bp coverage by 0.2–2.3 points; mostly a broken-vs-intact contrast.

Repeat 1: the Sp1 matrix matches it poorly (WT score 3.9); +0.09, q 0.15.

Repeat 2 (SRE-1): strong match (15.7) but no dose-response (−0.05, q 0.46).

Unmarked-fraction version: no motif at q < 0.05.

Source: a11_motifs_function/dose.tsv, dose_snvs.tsv · OLS with robust errors, A/T-dropping SNVs as a covariate · BH across motifs

Coverage at the site uses only adenines ≥ 6 bp from the SNV, but the site itself is within 10 bp of most of its SNVs, where A10 flags possible Hia5 sequence effects until B1. Few SNVs per motif have enough calls to test (15–29), so absence of a dose-response is weak evidence.
