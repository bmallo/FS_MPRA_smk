Step A7 · What the masks cost

The agreed mask changes the WT reference less than the ±5 bp mask

Symmetric masking removes the same adenines from WT, so its caller artifact is zero by construction. What it costs is a changed WT reference: calls near the variant shift by this much.

Agreed mask (edited base + re-contexted adenines): median 1.4% (HepG2), 1.5% (HEK293T); max 4.5–5.0%.

±5 bp mask: median 2.6–2.8%; max 6.7% (HepG2, oligo 181) and 8.0% (HEK293T, oligo 115–132).

Largest agreed-mask cost: 61A>T in HepG2 (4.5%; 7 adenines of the 59–65 A/T run masked) and the A at oligo 130 in HEK293T (5.0%; one adenine).

Source: a7_snv_edits/*/edits.tsv, variants.tsv (C molecules; M is smaller: medians 0.4–0.6% agreed, 0.8–1.1% ±5 bp)

"Largest change" = maximum over element positions and the four size bins of |edited − unedited| coverage. 93 distinct agreed-mask sets cover the 1,416 designed variants (most SNVs at G/C change nothing). Indels are placed at the designed index; the aligner may place a gap elsewhere in a run.
