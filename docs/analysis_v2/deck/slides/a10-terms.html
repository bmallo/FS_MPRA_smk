Step A10 · Terms used in this section

From one SNV's test to a list of hits

TermMeaning
FeatureOne adenine (E1, unmarked fraction) or one position × footprint size (E2, call coverage), tested SNV vs WT with A9's test.
Global test (Simes)One p-value per SNV: does it change anything? Combines all its feature p-values; its false-positive rate on WT fake variants was 4.9% (A9).
Selected SNVAn SNV whose global test passes FDR 5% across SNVs.
HitA feature of a selected SNV that passes FDR within that SNV, at 5% × (share of SNVs selected) (Benjamini–Bogomolov 2014).
A7 maskAdenines removed from SNV and WT alike: the edited base when it is an A/T, plus neighbours whose A-run context changes.
±5 bp maskPrespecified check: every adenine within 5 bp of the SNV removed, against Hia5's sequence preference nearby.
Empirical nullPost hoc check: far from each SNV, z values spread 13% wider than chance; all z are shrunk by that before FDR (Efron 2004).
Drops its baseSNVs that remove an A/T the caller reads (303 of 633); tested apart because A7 could not explain their offset.
PointsPercentage points of molecules: SNV minus WT, at matched labeling.

Settings: rules.json (variant_effects; statistics.amendment_3 for the Simes global test)

The global test was changed from "max |z| against pseudo-variants" to Simes before any A10 result: with 2,520 pseudo-variants the max-|z| p-value cannot fall below ~4×10⁻⁴, too coarse for FDR across ~300 SNVs.
