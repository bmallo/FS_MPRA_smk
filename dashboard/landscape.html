How read counts are normalized

At each position, we first calculate the fraction of eligible molecules with a covering call within each barcode. We then average those fractions across barcodes, giving every barcode equal weight. WT and each variant are calculated separately. A barcode with 20 reads therefore has the same total weight as one with 2 reads.

These are normalized coverage fractions, not raw counts. WT and variants are not downsampled to equal read counts, and lower barcode/read coverage still means less information. There is no additional normalization for overall methylation or chromatin state. Published MPRA values are displayed as provided by their source; this viewer does not recompute RNA/DNA normalization.

Interpretation and exports

Use coherent patterns to nominate regions and variants for subsequent audits. A/T count preservation does not guarantee unchanged sequence context. Changes in covered fraction are not instantaneous binding occupancy, and non-detection is not proof of absence.
