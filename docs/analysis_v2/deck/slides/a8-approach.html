Step A8 · Confidence in footprint calls

Does the caller find a footprint we know is there, and do WT profiles repeat?

Why

A variant test is only as good as the caller's sensitivity at that spot and the stability of the WT reference. Without naked controls we cannot measure false calls, but we can plant known footprints into real molecules and check repeatability.

What we did

Spike-ins: on real WT molecules, made every adenine in a chosen stretch unmarked (8 sizes, 10–147 bp, centred at every element position), re-called, compared with the same molecule unspiked. Repeatability: WT profiles in two halves split by barcode clone, and HepG2 run 1 vs run 3.

What we found

  • Well-labeled molecules: a fully protected 10-bp stretch is found 95% of the time; 20 bp at every position.
  • Poorly labeled molecules are already "called" almost everywhere, so they carry little footprint information.
  • Clone halves agree within sampling; runs 1 and 3 do not.
