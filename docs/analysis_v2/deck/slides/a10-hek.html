Step A10 · HEK293T · motif level

SNV clones differ from WT clones at most motifs; A/T-dropping SNVs go one way

Test: SNVs in each motif window pooled; each SNV clone's mean (≥ 100 molecules) against WT clones of similar depth (139), 10,000 draws.

Adenines: 7 of 9 tests pass FDR 5%. Drops-its-base pools lower in all 5 motifs; others higher at ZNF652-like (+1.6), SP4 (+3.7), SREBF1/2 (+3.8).

Calls (10–80 bp): 6 of 9 pass; −3.9 to +0.9 points.

Post hoc: the planned null (WT clones matched to each SNV's clone sizes) was invalid; each HEK SNV is one clone of up to 3,128 molecules and only 4–95 WT clones are that large, so its 1,000 draws reused the same few.

Source: a10_effects/HEK293T_run1/motifs.tsv · motif windows from the HepG2 v1 top-hit motif analysis · rules.json variant_effects.HEK293T_run1.amendment_1

Each SNV is ~one clone (median 99% of its molecules), so a pool of 3–15 SNVs is 2–15 independent units: these are motif-level, not per-SNV, results.
