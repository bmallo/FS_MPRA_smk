Follow-up · The footprint at oligo 60-86 EXPLORATORY

Is the 20-40 bp footprint between ZNF652-like and Repeat 1 real?

Why

A WT 20-40 bp footprint covers 60-86, mostly outside known motifs, with mild activity changes. It could be an unknown binder, or the caller reading an A/T-poor stretch next to an A run. A sharp WT − variant spike at 86 (86T>G, T>A) in the explorer added to the doubt.

What we did

Mapped the evidence each adenine carries; re-called WT molecules with the A run hidden; compared coverage across labeling with naked DNA simulated from this sequence; checked the 86 spike against the masked tests and A7; laid out every variant at 55-91.

What we found

  • A real protected core over ~66-81; its 20-40 bp size is stretched by the A run and the TT at 85-86.
  • The 86 spike is a caller artifact in the unmasked display; masking removes it.
  • Mild link to activity; the strongest activity losses nearby are at 55-62.
