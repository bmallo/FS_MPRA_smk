Step A11 · Motifs and function

Do chromatin changes follow motif damage, and do they go with expression?

Why

A10 found where SNVs change protection. Two checks make those changes meaningful: a bound factor's footprint should shrink as its motif is broken, and chromatin changes that matter should go with changes in expression.

What we did

Motifs: the three LDLR promoter repeats from the literature (Sp1, SRE-1, Sp1); each SNV's motif-score change against the footprint change there. Function: each SNV's chromatin change against its published expression effect (Kircher 2019, HepG2, two measurements), comparing only alleles at the same position.

What we found

  • Breaking the Repeat 3 Sp1 site lowers TF-size footprints there; the other repeats show no dose-response.
  • SNVs that raise protection across the element lower expression, even among alleles at one position.
  • The 17 Repeat 3 SNVs: ~6-fold lower expression.
