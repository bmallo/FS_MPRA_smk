Barcodes shared by two clones (run 3)

2,964 of 1.01 M barcodes with ≥ 2 reads (0.3%) hold two well-supported genotypes. Random sharing of 15-mers among 1.63 M barcodes predicts about 1,240, so most are chance, with some template switching.

Source: a3_genotypes/cross_run_barcodes.tsv, second_genotyper.tsv, barcode_collisions.tsv

Cross-run disagreements split about evenly between one-edit differences and unrelated genotypes (run 1 vs 3: 23 and 24 of 7,381). The two genotypers compare element sequences, so differences in how they name homopolymer indels do not count as disagreements. The Illumina barcode-amplicon data could not serve as a third check: its read pairs rarely span both element and barcode (1,282 complete pairs).
