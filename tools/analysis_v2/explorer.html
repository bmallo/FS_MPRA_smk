Molecules: 40 sampled per track (labeling ≥ 0.2), lowest labeling at the bottom; bars are footprint calls clipped to the element, dots are m6A-marked adenines. Coverage: share of molecules with labeling ≥ 0.2 covered by a call of that size. Unmarked: share of molecules (labeling > 0.01) where the adenine has no m6A. WT tracks are weighted to the variant's labeling mix, so WT − variant is exactly the difference of the two tracks. The footprint difference defaults to the A10 test with the chosen mask (bands ±1.96 SE): "as read" shows the unmasked difference, which still contains caller artifacts where a variant removes an A/T (e.g. a one-position spike at 86 for 86T>X). Unmarked differences and all significance markers come from the same masked test. With "same axes" on, values beyond ±8 (footprints) or ±25 points (unmarked) run off the panel; turn it off to rescale. The grey band marks ±10 bp around the variant, where changes may be Hia5 sequence preference until naked controls.
