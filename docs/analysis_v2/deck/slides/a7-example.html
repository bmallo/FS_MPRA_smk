Step A7 · One edit, two real molecules

Removing one adenine can merge calls or erase one

Left: the marked adenine at oligo 137 pulled the score down between two protected stretches. Without it the score never falls back, so a 17-bp and a 34-bp call become one 68-bp call.

Right: the unmarked adenine at oligo 120 was part of a weak 12-bp call. Without it the peak drops from 5.9 to 4.9, under the threshold of 5, and the call is gone.

Nothing about the molecule changed: only the caller's evidence. A7 repeats this on every WT molecule, for every edit, and counts how coverage moves.

Source: tools/analysis_v2/a7_example.py · HepG2 run 3 C molecules (A6 rows 1090 and 8), run-3 fold-A emissions · found by scanning for a clear merge and a clear loss

These two molecules were chosen to illustrate the two mechanisms; most single drops change nothing on most molecules. Across all molecules the largest coverage change per drop has a median of 0.9% of C molecules (next slides). Call lengths are the full calls; the window shows ±45 bp around the dropped base.
