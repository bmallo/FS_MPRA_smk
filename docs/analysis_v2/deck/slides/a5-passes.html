The minimum of 10 passes stays, set by genotype errors in A2–A3, not by m6A quality: below 10 passes, the U call rate is 0.35–0.53% and the M rate 62–66%, close to higher bins. The labeling–passes link is consistent with heavy m6A slowing the polymerase, but we have not tested the cause. The A3 rule for indels at ≥ 20 passes therefore needs WT at the same filter and a reported class mix.
