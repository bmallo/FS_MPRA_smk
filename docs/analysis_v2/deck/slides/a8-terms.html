Step A8 · Terms used in this section

Planting a footprint we know is there, and measuring twice

TermMeaning
LabelingShare of a molecule's backbone adenines that carry m6A: how hard Hia5 hit that molecule, independent of the element.
M / C moleculesM: labeling ≥ 0.6 (heavily marked). C: 0.01–0.6, split here into low (0.01–0.2), mid (0.2–0.4) and high (0.4–0.6).
Spike-inA fake footprint: on a real WT molecule, every adenine in a chosen stretch is set unmarked, as if a protein had blocked Hia5 there. The molecule is re-called.
DetectedAfter the spike, a call covers the spike's centre and at least half of it.
Already calledA call covered that spot before anything was spiked.
Net detectionDetected after the spike − already called before, on the same molecules: the part the spike itself caused.
Leaky spikeOnly 3 of 4 adenines in the stretch blocked (75% protection).
Clone halvesWT molecules split in two by barcode clone, each clone wholly in one half; the same profile computed in each.
z and SD(z)Per position: (half 1 − half 2) ÷ the expected chance difference. If only chance separates them, the z values spread with SD ≈ 1 and ~5% fall beyond ±1.96.

Caller as in A7: plasmid-LLR, min LLR 5, drawdown 25 · settings in rules.json (call_confidence)

"Expected chance difference" comes from a bootstrap that resamples whole barcode clones (200 draws), so molecules of one clone move together. SD(z) above 1 means the two profiles differ by more than sampling explains; below 1 means the error bars are wider than needed.
