Sequence and candidate motif context

Green letters/ticks mark reference A/T opportunities; orange marks the selected substitution. An A↔T substitution retains the count but changes the strand carrying adenine. Motif arrows show motif orientation on the genomic forward axis.

Candidate sequence matches

All matches in this view are listed; the canvas shows at most eight. Tail probabilities describe random-sequence matches under a specified background, without multiple-testing correction. They are not evidence of binding or variant significance. Δ score is the selected substitution's change at the same WT site, not a search for new sites. Correlations (ρ) compare motif-score changes with each phenotype across all measured substitutions overlapping the motif. They are descriptive and do not establish protein identity.
