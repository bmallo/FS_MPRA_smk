Step A3 · Genotype and barcode audit

Are reads assigned to the right variant and the right clone?

Why

A wrong genotype mixes WT molecules into a variant and dilutes its effect. A wrong clone grouping makes reads of one plasmid look like independent replicates and overstates confidence.

What we did

Wrote down the rules. Used barcodes as truth: all reads of one barcode come from one plasmid clone. Checked the same barcode across independent runs, compared a second, separately written genotyper, and tested barcode rules.

What we found

  • Genotype calls are reproducible: 99.4–99.98% across runs, 99.99% across genotypers.
  • 1.6% of WT-clone reads are misread as a variant, mostly homopolymer indels.
  • A majority barcode rule fixes deep clones.

Source: results/analysis_v2_20261028/a3_genotypes (tools/analysis_v2/a3_genotype_audit.py) · WT, SNVs and designed 1-bp indels

All three HepG2 runs come from the same physical plasmid library, so a barcode seen in two runs is the same clone, sequenced and transfected independently. That gives an external check on genotype calls without any extra experiment.
