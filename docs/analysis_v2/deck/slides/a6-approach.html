Step A6 · What M and U can stand in for

Are the high-labeling (M) molecules free of protein over the element?

Why

The LLR caller has no naked-DNA control. It treats WT M molecules as "accessible" and U as "protected". If M carries footprints, the caller absorbs them and loses power exactly there.

What we did

Took every A/T call on about 250,000 WT molecules. Asked whether unmethylated adenines cluster into m6A-free stretches more than a shuffle of the same calls predicts. Compared the element with the backbone tiles of the same molecules and with naked DNA.

What we found

  • M is the top slice of a labeling continuum, at the level of naked DNA.
  • M is not protein-free: 1–2% of M molecules carry nucleosome-sized stretches; naked DNA ~0.
  • The element is typical, apart from one spot (oligo 60–86, 6–11% of M).
